Introduction

An excellent opportunity to acquire a bright and generously proportioned two-bedroom apartment in the popular Tannery development on Cork Street. Situated on the first floor and enjoying its own-door entrance, this impressive apartment offers well-balanced accommodation with excellent natural light throughout. The property benefits from particularly large windows, allowing natural light to stream into the apartment and creating a bright, welcoming atmosphere. The heart of the home is the large open-plan living, dining and kitchen area, providing an excellent space for both everyday living and entertaining. From the living area, doors open directly onto a large private balcony, extending the living space outdoors and providing a pleasant area to relax or entertain.

The master bedroom is generously proportioned and features its own en-suite bathroom, as well as direct access to the balcony — a particularly attractive feature. The second bedroom is also a good-sized double bedroom and is complemented by a separate main bathroom. A generous laundry/storage room provides valuable additional storage and practical utility space, helping to keep the apartment well organised. The accommodation is complemented by the apartment's excellent sense of space and natural brightness, with the large windows and dual-aspect access to the balcony enhancing the overall feeling of openness. Accommodation briefly comprises own-door entrance, entrance hall, large open-plan living/dining area, kitchen, large balcony, two double bedrooms (master en-suite), main bathroom and generous laundry/storage room. An attractive property offering bright, spacious and well-proportioned accommodation in a convenient Dublin 8 location, which should appeal to both owner-occupiers and investors alike.

Location

The location is exceptionally convenient, with the apartment situated on Cork Street in the heart of Dublin 8 and Lidl located immediately beside the development, making the weekly grocery shop quite literally just moments from your front door. The area offers an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants, shops and everyday amenities, while Dublin city centre is within easy reach. There are also excellent transport connections nearby, including the Luas Red Line and a range of Dublin Bus routes, providing easy access throughout the city. The location is further enhanced by its proximity to St James's Hospital, the Coombe Hospital and the National Children's Hospital, making it particularly appealing to medical and those working in the surrounding area.
